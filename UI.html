Presses

PadAction
STEP nToggles the note on step n of the selected pattern.
VOICE vSelects voice v for editing, and lights up while it fires.
COPYCopies the selected pattern to the paste buffer.
PASTEOverwrites the selected pattern with the paste buffer.
CLEARZeroes every step of the selected pattern.
UNDOSteps back through pattern edits, 256 patterns deep. Each edit is restored to the voice and pattern slot it came from, whether or not that is what the grid is showing. A swap or an all-voice transform undoes in one press. TRANSFORM + UNDO redoes.

Held modifiers

CombinationEffect
ACCENT + STEP nAccents step n (velocity 127). An accented step goes back to normal velocity, a silent step switches on accented. Never switches a step off.
ACCENT + CLEARRemoves every accent from the selected pattern, leaving the notes in place.
LEN + STEP nSets the selected pattern’s length to n steps.
POS + STEP nMoves the selected voice’s play head to step n.
POS + ALL + STEP nMoves every voice’s play head to step n.
VOICE v + STEP nSelects pattern n for voice v without toggling the step. Several voice pads can be held at once.
ALL + STEP nSelects pattern n for all six voices.
TRANSFORM + …Pattern transforms, see below.
TRANSFORM + ALL + …The same transforms on every voice at once, see below.

Transforms

The pad map while TRANSFORM is held. Step pads apply an edit to the selected pattern, voice pads swap patterns between voices (the selected voice’s own pad shifts its note instead), UNDO turns into redo and PASTE pastes into every pattern slot of the selected voice. ACCENT is a prefix: held as well, it changes what the step pads do. Holding ALL as well applies the step pads to every voice, see the next section. Every edit acts within the pattern length and leaves steps past it alone.

PadEffect
STEP 1, 2Shifts the pattern left or right by 1 step, wrapping within its length.
STEP 3, 4Shifts the pattern left or right by 4 steps, wrapping within its length.
STEP 5Reverses the pattern.
STEP 6Inverts the pattern: sounding steps go silent and silent steps start sounding.
STEP 7Spreads the sounding steps evenly across the length (a Euclidean rhythm), keeping their velocities in order.
STEP 8Fill empty: replaces the pattern with notes on every step where no other voice sounds. Busy voices count for less, so a hi-hat on every step does not block anything. If there is no free step, it places as many notes as the sparsest other voice has on steps drawn at random from the least crowded ones, so each press gives a different fill. It never copies another voice outright.
STEP 9Shuffles the steps into a random order.
STEP 10Echo: every note gets a copy two steps later at half its velocity, wrapping at the pattern length. A copy never replaces a louder step, so the original notes and accents stay. Press again and the echoes echo, one more repeat per press, each half as loud.
STEP 11Mutate: moves one note one step left or right, chosen at random among the moves that land on a step where this voice is silent and no other voice sounds. A voice that sounds on every step blocks nothing, as with fill empty. The note keeps its velocity. Press repeatedly to let a groove drift while it keeps out of the other parts’ way; when no move fits, nothing changes. With ALL this pad is Declutter instead, see below.
STEP 12Rule 30: advances the pattern one generation of the Rule 30 cellular automaton, a chaotic one-dimensional rule where each step’s fate is decided by itself and its two neighbours, with the pattern read as a ring. The result is trimmed back to the number of notes the pattern had, keeping the steps where the fewest other voices sound and breaking ties at random, so the voice keeps its density and never falls silent. Steps that keep sounding keep their velocity. With ALL this pad is Life instead, see below.
ACCENT + STEP nAccents every n-th sounding step counting from step 1 and drops every other accent. STEP 4 accents the downbeats, STEP 2 the on-beats, STEP 1 every note. Silent steps are left alone.
VOICE vSwaps the selected pattern with voice v’s current pattern.
VOICE v, selectedMoves voice v’s MIDI note up 6 semitones, or back. Not a pattern edit, so undo leaves it alone.
UNDORedoes the last undone edit. Any new edit after an undo discards what could have been redone.
PASTEOverwrites all 16 pattern slots of the selected voice with the paste buffer, as one undo step.

Row one shifts, by one step on the first pair of pads and by four on the second, left then right. Row two holds the deterministic reshapes and row three shuffle, echo, mutate and rule 30. Row four is free. Every transform is a single undo step, including swaps.

Transforms on all voices

The pad map while TRANSFORM and ALL are both held. Every step pad applies its transform to each voice’s current pattern, as one undo group, so one press of UNDO brings all six back. The transforms run voice by voice in voice order, which matters for the ones that read the other voices: fill empty, mutate and rule 30 each see the voices before them already changed. Declutter and Life are the exceptions, they act on the whole board at once. LEN deals odd lengths to the voices. The other pads do what they do under TRANSFORM alone.

PadEffect
STEP 1–7, 9, 10The transform of the same pad under TRANSFORM alone, applied to every voice’s current pattern.
STEP 8Fill empty for every voice in turn. Voice 0 fills the gaps the other five leave, voice 1 then fills around voice 0’s new pattern and the rest, and so on, so the result is a set of interlocking parts rather than six copies of the same fill.
STEP 11Declutter: on every step where two or more voices sound, silences one of them, chosen at random. Each press thins the pile-ups by one voice, so a step with three voices takes two presses, and once no step has more than one voice the pad does nothing. Steps are compared as the grid shows them, each voice within its own length. The random choice means two presses from the same kit give different results.
STEP 12Life: advances all six voices one generation of Conway’s Game of Life, with voices as rows and steps as columns. A step survives with two or three sounding neighbours and keeps its velocity, an empty step with exactly three is born, everything else falls silent. Rows wrap at their own length, the first and last voice are neighbours, and every row is computed from the board as it was before the press. Life needs the whole board to stay alive, a single row against a frozen board dies or freezes within a press or two, which is why this pad runs Rule 30 without ALL.
ACCENT + STEP nAccents every n-th sounding step of every voice’s current pattern and drops every other accent, as under TRANSFORM + ACCENT.
LENOdd meters: deals the six odd lengths 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 to the six voices in random order, so every voice gets a different one and the parts drift against each other. The steps are untouched, so undo, or LEN + STEP 16, brings a pattern back whole. One undo group.
VOICE v, UNDO, PASTEAs under TRANSFORM alone: swap with voice v, redo, paste into every slot of the selected voice. ALL makes no difference to these.
