Presses

PadAction
STEP nToggles the note on step n of the selected pattern.
VOICE vSelects voice v for editing, and lights up while it fires. Clears the undo buffer.
COPYCopies the selected pattern to the paste buffer.
PASTEOverwrites the selected pattern with the paste buffer.
CLEARZeroes every step of the selected pattern.
UNDOSteps back through pattern edits (16 levels).

Held modifiers

CombinationEffect
LEN + STEP nSets the selected pattern’s length to n steps.
POS + STEP nMoves the selected voice’s play head to step n.
POS + ALL + STEP nMoves every voice’s play head to step n.
VOICE v + STEP nSelects pattern n for voice v without toggling the step. Several voice pads can be held at once.
ALL + STEP nSelects pattern n for all six voices.
TRANSFORM + STEP 1–4Shifts the selected pattern right by 1–4 steps, wrapping within its length.
TRANSFORM + STEP 5–8Shifts the selected pattern left by 1–4 steps, wrapping within its length.
TRANSFORM + STEP 9Reverses the selected pattern within its length.
TRANSFORM + STEP 10Inverts the selected pattern: sounding steps go silent and silent steps start sounding.
TRANSFORM + STEP 11Spreads the selected pattern’s sounding steps evenly across its length (a Euclidean rhythm), keeping their velocities in order.
TRANSFORM + STEP 12Unassigned.
TRANSFORM + STEP 13Shuffles the steps of the selected pattern into a random order.
TRANSFORM + STEP 14–16Unassigned.

Colours

ConstantUsed for
COLOR_OFFUnassigned pads, and steps past the pattern length
COLOR_VOCnVoice select pads, one hue per voice
COLOR_VOCn_SETSteps with a note, in the selected voice’s hue
COLOR_VOCn_UNSETEmpty steps, in the selected voice’s hue
COLOR_PACTCopy, paste, clear, undo
COLOR_PMODLength, position, transform
COLOR_PPOSPlay head, voices firing this step, and the ALL pad

Values live in src/colors.h, generated from contrib/palette.go. The step grid above is drawn in voice 0’s hue; on the device it follows the selected voice.
