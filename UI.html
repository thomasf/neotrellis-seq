Presses

PadAction
STEP nToggles the note on step n of the selected pattern.
VOICE vSelects voice v for editing, and lights up while it fires.
COPYCopies the selected pattern to the paste buffer.
PASTEOverwrites the selected pattern with the paste buffer.
CLEARZeroes every step of the selected pattern.
UNDOSteps back through pattern edits, 256 patterns deep. Each edit is restored to the voice and pattern slot it came from, whether or not that is what the grid is showing. A swap or an all-voice transform undoes in one press. TRANSFORM + UNDO redoes.

Held modifiers

CombinationEffect
ACCENT + STEP nAccents step n (velocity 127). An accented step goes back to normal velocity, a silent step switches on accented. Never switches a step off.
ACCENT + CLEARRemoves every accent from the selected pattern, leaving the notes in place.
LEN + STEP nSets the selected pattern’s length to n steps.
POS + STEP nMoves the selected voice’s play head to step n.
POS + ALL + STEP nMoves every voice’s play head to step n.
VOICE v + STEP nSelects pattern n for voice v without toggling the step. Several voice pads can be held at once.
ALL + STEP nSelects pattern n for all six voices.
TRANSFORM + …Pattern transforms, see below.

Transforms

The pad map while TRANSFORM is held. Step pads apply an edit to the selected pattern, voice pads swap patterns between voices (the selected voice’s own pad shifts its note instead), UNDO turns into redo and PASTE pastes into every pattern slot of the selected voice. ALL and ACCENT are prefixes: held as well, they change what the step pads do. Every edit acts within the pattern length and leaves steps past it alone.

PadEffect
STEP 1–4Shifts the pattern right by 1–4 steps, wrapping within its length.
STEP 5–8Shifts the pattern left by 1–4 steps, wrapping within its length.
STEP 9Reverses the pattern.
STEP 10Inverts the pattern: sounding steps go silent and silent steps start sounding.
STEP 11Spreads the sounding steps evenly across the length (a Euclidean rhythm), keeping their velocities in order.
STEP 12Fill empty: replaces the pattern with notes on every step where no other voice sounds. Busy voices count for less, so a hi-hat on every step does not block anything. If there is no free step, it places as many notes as the sparsest other voice has on steps drawn at random from the least crowded ones, so each press gives a different fill. It never copies another voice outright.
STEP 13Shuffles the steps into a random order.
STEP 16Rule 30: advances the pattern one generation of the Rule 30 cellular automaton, a chaotic one-dimensional rule where each step’s fate is decided by itself and its two neighbours, with the pattern read as a ring. The result is trimmed back to the number of notes the pattern had, keeping the steps where the fewest other voices sound and breaking ties at random, so the voice keeps its density and never falls silent. Steps that keep sounding keep their velocity.
ALL + STEP nApplies transform n to every voice’s current pattern instead of just the selected one.
ALL + STEP 16Life: advances all six voices one generation of Conway’s Game of Life, with voices as rows and steps as columns. A step survives with two or three sounding neighbours and keeps its velocity, an empty step with exactly three is born, everything else falls silent. Rows wrap at their own length, the first and last voice are neighbours, and every row is computed from the board before the press. One undo brings the whole board back. Life needs the whole board to stay alive, which is why the key alone runs Rule 30 instead.
ACCENT + STEP nAccents every n-th sounding step counting from step 1 and drops every other accent. STEP 4 accents the downbeats, STEP 2 the on-beats, STEP 1 every note. Silent steps are left alone.
ACCENT + ALL + STEP nThe same, for every voice’s current pattern.
VOICE vSwaps the selected pattern with voice v’s current pattern.
VOICE v, selectedMoves voice v’s MIDI note up 6 semitones, or back. Not a pattern edit, so undo leaves it alone.
UNDORedoes the last undone edit. Any new edit after an undo discards what could have been redone.
PASTEOverwrites all 16 pattern slots of the selected voice with the paste buffer, as one undo step.

Rows one and two shift, row three holds the deterministic reshapes and row four the random ones. Every transform is a single undo step, including swaps and all-voice transforms.
